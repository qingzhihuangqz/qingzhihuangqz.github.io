About

Building bridges from human demos to robot action

My current focus is egocentric hand–object interaction for robot manipulation: recovering hand and object motion from first-person video, bridging embodiment and physics gaps, and connecting real capture pipelines with simulation.

I care about practical full-stack systems — from data capture and 3D estimation to real-to-sim transfer, trajectory retargeting, and sim-to-real deployment.
