I am interested in robot learning and 3D perception for manipulation, especially how egocentric human videos can be turned into usable robot skills.

About

My current focus is egocentric hand–object interaction (HOI) for robot manipulation: recovering hand and object motion from first-person video, bridging the embodiment and physics gaps between humans and robots, and connecting real capture pipelines with simulation.

I care about practical full-stack systems — from data capture and 3D estimation to real-to-sim transfer, trajectory retargeting, and sim-to-real deployment.

Research Interests

  • Robot learning from human demonstration and egocentric video
  • Hand–object interaction understanding and 3D reconstruction
  • Egocentric 3D hand pose and object 6-DoF estimation
  • Real-to-sim / sim-to-real for dexterous and desktop manipulation
  • Motion retargeting from human hands to robot embodiments
  • Digital twin construction for tabletop interaction scenes

Selected Topics
